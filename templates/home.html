All the house rules

Aces are 11, face cards are 10, and number cards use their value. Only cards of the same suit add together. Three of a kind has no special score. All players tied for lowest lose one life. A 31 ends the round immediately and every player without 31 loses one life. There is no extra “on the county” life. Knocking replaces your turn. A picked-up discard may be discarded again. Eliminated players can watch. The host deals each new round. Refreshing on the same browser keeps your seat.

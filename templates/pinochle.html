FOUR PLAYERS. TWO PARTNERSHIPS.

Pinochle.
Better together.

Bid with your partner, find your meld, and take the tricks. An 80-card double deck, and a seat for each of you.

Your table, your rules.

Seats fill in join order. Seats 1 & 3 are Team 1; seats 2 & 4 are Team 2. Invite your partner to join third if you are hosting.

Default rules

20 cards each; no nines. Rank: A, 10, K, Q, J. Bid from 50; bids above 60 go in fives. A pass is final; if the first three pass, the dealer takes 50. The bidder chooses a suit with a marriage (K + Q) and leads first. No card passing by default.

Meld is calculated and shown automatically. Follow suit, otherwise trump; beat the current winner when possible, even if your partner is winning. Equal cards favor the first played. Aces, tens and kings count 1; the last trick adds 2. A team must take a trick to count its meld. Missing the bid costs the bid amount. First to 500 wins; if both reach it together, the bidding side wins.

Runs: 15 / 150 / 225 / 300. Pinochles: 4 / 30 / 60 / 90. Arounds: A 10 / 100 / 150 / 200; K 8 / 80 / 120 / 160; Q 6 / 60 / 90 / 120; J 4 / 40 / 60 / 80. Marriages score 4 in trump or 2 otherwise; a run already includes its marriage.

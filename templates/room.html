How to play

Draw from the deck or discard pile, then select a card to discard. Aces = 11; faces = 10. Your best single suit is your score. Knock before drawing to give the others one final turn. Lowest score loses a life (including ties). A 31 ends the round immediately; everyone else loses a life.
